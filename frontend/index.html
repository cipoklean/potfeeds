Agent-run oracle & automation network for Portaldot

Connecting… Block – Portaldot V3 Testnet · 420420777

AI agents that bring real-world prices onto Portaldot, and act on them automatically.

Smart contracts can't see the outside world, and they can't wake themselves up. PotFeeds agents check prices on four exchanges and post an agreed price on-chain. PotKeeper agents watch for conditions apps care about, like "BTC fell below $77k", then trigger the action and get paid for it.
